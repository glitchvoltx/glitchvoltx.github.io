Description:

An original indie anime following Shion,a quiet high school The boy is secretly obsessed with romance anime,as he tries — and fails — to live out his favorite tropes in real life.

Characters:

REI HAIZAKI

Class: 3A

Role: Akari's Best Friend

Loud, crazy, and constantly jokes about Shion and Akari's relationship. But she secretly and deeply cares about everyone around her.

EMI HOSHINO

Class: 3B

Role: Student Council President

Strict, rule-following, hates Rei with a passion. Proper and composed.Proper and composed. May secretly love Shio. A love triangle… maybe.

AOI MURASAKI

Class: None (Adult)

Role: Shion's Mother

Suspiciously young looking.Knows about Shion's romance anime obsession and actively ENCOURAGES it. Probably the one who buys him the DVDs. Every character who meets her thinks she's his older sister.

KAITO TANAKA

Class: 3A

Role: Shion's Best Friend

LOUD. Energetic hype man. Always scheming, always has the worst ideas that somehow work. The one who dragged Shion into the double date situation.

SHION MURASAKI

Class: 3A

Role: Male Protagonist

Quiet and calm on the outside, medium grades. Secret hardcore romance anime otaku — knows every trope, tries to use them in real life, fails spectacularly every time.

AKARI AKABANE

Class: 3A

Role: Female Lead

Quiet and reserved on the outside, Akari hides a surprisingly fluffy and chaotic personality. Growing up with emotionally distant parents shaped her cynical view of romance. She avoids love entirely until her feelings slowly begin to change. Also, she loves J-rock and wants to be in a band in the future.

SUMIRE KUROKAWA

Class: 3B

Role: Akari's Friend

The sidekick. Sumire is a friend of Akari's which also happens to be Kaito's date . what a mess!
